# KH2 Co-op: master to-do list

Last updated: 2026-10-11 (session 27: forced-solo fights (5.5), cutscene skip and pause (5.3), the disconnect handling and the 'Connection lost' screen (6.1, 6.8) confirmed on two PCs and pushed). Before: 2026-10-10 (session 25: two save files and the LOAD (CO-OP) title row, Steam connection). This file is **the plan**: what's left, in what order, and what we're ignoring on purpose.
`MODLOG.md` stays the diary (what happened, with evidence) and `VERIFIED_OFFSETS.md` the address book.

## The goal: what "playable" means
Two players on two PCs play KH2FM **from Sora's first steps (right after the Roxas prologue) to the end of the final Xemnas fight**:
- no crashes or soft locks along the story route;
- every required fight can be fought together;
- cutscenes play on both screens (the host starts them);
- each player keeps **their own Sora**: level, HP, items, Keyblades, gear, abilities, munny;
- both saves move through the story together;
- parts that can't be co-op (minigames, Gummi Ship, a few forced-solo scenes) don't break anything. The second player waits, then gets pulled back in automatically.

## How to read this file
- **P0** = blocks the start-to-finish run. **P1** = needed for it to play well. **P2** = polish / later. **Parked** = ignored on purpose for now (see "Parked").
- `[ ]` open, `[~]` built but not tested yet, `[x]` done. **(Qn)** = follows decision Qn in "Decisions".
- Numbers (1.3, 4.2...) stay fixed, so we can say "let's do 4.2 next". Tracks 1 to 4 are the four priorities you and your friend agreed on, in that order.
- **Host** = the PC that owns the world (yours). **Friend** = the second PC. **Copy** = the other player's Sora as it appears in your game.

## Where we are (end of session 27, 2026-10-11)
Working, tested on two PCs (over Steam or Radmin) or on two game copies on one PC:
- **Connecting:** Steam (Join Game from the friends list, or the host's Steam ID typed in) or Radmin/IP, with a settings handshake. Both players start from LOAD (CO-OP) on the title screen.
- **Saves:** the title has NEW GAME / LOAD / LOAD (CO-OP) / BACK. LOAD = the normal Steam save with the whole mod off; LOAD (CO-OP) = a separate co-op save file next to the game (`KH2 Coop saves`) with the mod on. The Steam save is never written in co-op, and co-op saves never carry the Sora copy.
- **Each player is Sora in their own game.** The other player shows up as a Sora copy in the party, replacing the active Donald or Goofy (the world's guest ally stays). It mirrors movement, attacks, magic, items and Limits (a Drive Form still shows as a normal Sora, 1.24), has its own party HUD widget, and the host's Sora wears the gold crown so the two can be told apart.
- **The host owns rooms and enemies.** The friend follows every room change (with a load barrier so both come out of the black screen together); enemy spawns, positions, motion, HP and deaths are mirrored; hits from either player count; EXP and drops reach both.
- **Story:** cutscenes play on both, with a shared pause and the host's Skip Scene; walk-in story scenes can be started by either player; the other Sora appears in story cutscenes; forced-solo fights (Sora alone, like Bailey) have both players fight together without companions.
- **Downed and Game Over:** downed state (30 s by default, Mickey-rescue animations), revive with Cure or items, both down = Game Over, and the choice follows the host. Shared combat pause. Steam achievements are blocked in co-op.
- **Disconnects:** if one game closes, crashes or loses the connection, the other freezes on a 'Connection lost. Reconnecting to Host/Client...' screen with **Continue Alone** (Donald and Goofy come back; the room reloads, fights and bosses start over) and **Title Screen**. When the other player comes back, co-op resumes and the room reloads with the Sora copy.
- **Story route played in co-op:** Twilight Town and the Mysterious Tower, on to Hollow Bastion's first visit through the Bailey fight. Earlier tests: Olympus up to and including Cerberus, Beast's Castle (party only), the 100 Acre Wood entrance.
- Tools: save guard, two-copy launcher, virtual pads, scene scripts, debug commands, effect-pool monitor, friend packager.

Still open: everything past Bailey on the route; boss-fight disconnect edge cases (6.1b); summons; the world map and story-progress sync between the two saves; the dev suite. See "Suggested order" at the end.
